# Privacy Policy

### 1. Introduction
This Privacy Policy describes how **Lispsinto Chat** handles user information. Our application serves as a frontend client for the **lisp-chat** protocol ([https://github.com/ryukinix/lisp-chat](https://github.com/ryukinix/lisp-chat)). By using this app, you acknowledge that it is an interface tool and that all communication occurs through third-party servers. 

**Lispinto Chat** is an independent client and is neither the official nor the sole method of accessing the chat protocol.

### 2. Data Collection by the App (Frontend)
Designed with privacy at its core, **Lispinto Chat** operates under the following principles:
* **No Accounts:** Users are not required to create accounts, provide email addresses, or set passwords. Access is granted via anonymous nicknames chosen by the user.
* **Local Storage:** All server configurations and message logs processed by the app are stored **exclusively on your local device**. We do not collect, sell, or transmit your personal data to our own servers.

### 3. Third-Party Servers and Protocol
Users have the freedom to configure and connect to any server that supports the **lisp-chat** protocol. 
* **User Responsibility:** We have no control over the data retention policies, logs, or privacy practices of the servers chosen by the user.
* **Server-Side Data:** Any server selected by the user may store sent messages and will have access to the user's IP address to facilitate the technical connection. We strongly recommend that users verify the trustworthiness of the server they choose to connect to.

### 4. Permissions
The application requests only the permissions strictly necessary for its operation:
* **Internet:** Required to connect to the chat servers selected by the user.
* **Push notifications:** On desktop and web, you may optionally give permission for the app to show push notifications when you are mentioned in the chat or when you receive server notifications.

### 5. Data Retention and Deletion
Since data is stored locally, you can delete your information at any time by clearing the application cache or uninstalling the app. For data sent to chat servers, users must consult the administrator of that specific server.

### 6. Children's Privacy
This application does not intentionally collect personally identifiable data from children. As there is no login system, we do not monitor or verify the age of our users.

### 7. Changes to This Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We recommend reviewing this page periodically for any changes.
